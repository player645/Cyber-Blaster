CYBER BLASTER

ARCADE MANUAL

Light-gun arcade shooter: Eliminate hostiles, protect Hostages, manage ammo & threat timers, survive waves, and chase the highest score!

CONTROLS & OBJECTIVE

SHOOT Click / Tap on targets
RELOAD Press SPACE / R or tap RELOAD button
Clear all hostiles before the threat timer expires to advance waves!

TARGET SHOWCASE

EXPOSED / COVER / STATIONARY (1 Shot • 100 PTS)
Standard hostile targets appearing in static, moving, or cover pop-up patterns.
ARMORED ENEMIES (Multi-Shot • Toughness PTS)
Toughness: Yellow (2), Green (3), Blue (4), Violet (5). Points awarded only on full elimination (Base 100 × Toughness).
HOSTAGE (-1 Life • -250 PTS)
DO NOT SHOOT! Shooting a hostage causes life loss and immediate wave reset.

SCORING & COMBO METER

• SCORING: Base Points × Combo Multiplier (Round(Combo^1.5)). Hostage hit = -250 PTS.

• COMBO BUILDING: Eliminations increase combo up to x5 (x10 with 2x Multiplier).

• ARMORED HITS: Damaging Armored Enemies refreshes combo timer without increasing combo level. Elimination increases combo level.

• COMBO BREAK: Missed shots or hostage hits reset combo to 0.
